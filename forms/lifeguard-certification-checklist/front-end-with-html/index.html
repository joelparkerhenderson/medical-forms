Use this checklist while observing a candidate complete the practical lifeguard certification assessment. For each item, mark whether the candidate demonstrated the skill correctly, has not yet demonstrated it, or whether the item was not assessed in this session. Critical competencies are highlighted; any critical-competency failure results in an overall Fail.

Outcome rules: any critical-competency failure is an automatic Fail. With no critical failures, one or two non-critical deficiencies produce Needs Development; otherwise the candidate passes. Answers are saved automatically to your browser as you go.

The Lifeguard Competency Verification Checklist is aligned with the RLSS UK National Pool Lifeguard Qualification (NPLQ) and the International Life Saving Federation (ILSF) competency framework. This examiner tool records observed performance and applies the published pass/fail criteria; it is not a substitute for the examiner's professional judgement.
