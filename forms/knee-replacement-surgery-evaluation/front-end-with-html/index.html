Orthopaedic-surgeon or extended-scope-physiotherapist consultation record covering presenting history, the twelve-item Oxford Knee Score, functional limitations, a physical examination (range of motion, stability and alignment, muscle strength and effusion), diagnostic imaging, and a conservative-treatment audit. The engine computes an Oxford Knee Score total and category, a surgical- candidacy recommendation, and a set of safety flags. The draft is saved to this browser as you work, so a partly-filled evaluation survives a page reload.

This form does not compute an ASA grade or an anaesthesia plan — step 11 is a high-level general-health screen only. It also does not replace a formal pre-operative assessment.

This is clinical decision support. It does not make a diagnosis and does not replace the clinical judgement of the orthopaedic surgeon or extended-scope physiotherapist, who must sign the evaluation before the report is final.

Scoring follows the Oxford Knee Score (Dawson, Fitzpatrick, Murray & Carr, J Bone Joint Surg Br 1998) and the Kellgren-Lawrence radiographic grading system (Kellgren & Lawrence, Ann Rheum Dis 1957). This tool is for clinical decision support; the surgical-candidacy recommendation and management plan remain the responsibility of the orthopaedic surgeon or extended-scope physiotherapist who signs the evaluation.
