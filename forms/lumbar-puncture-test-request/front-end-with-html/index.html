Clinician referral wizard for a lumbar puncture (LP) for CSF sampling and/or opening-pressure manometry. Record the procedure intent, the clinical indication and specific question, the raised-intracranial-pressure and bleeding-risk safety screening, the opening-pressure requirement, and the requested urgency. The engine computes a four-axis grade (appropriateness, safety / contraindication, request completeness, and triage priority) plus a set of safety flags.

This is clinical decision support for the vetting desk. The final triage and scheduling decision remains the responsibility of the vetting neurology / acute-medicine clinician.

Submit the form to see the vetting report.

Grading is aligned with NICE NG240 (bacterial meningitis — neuroimaging before LP), subarachnoid-haemorrhage / CSF-xanthochromia guidance, NICE QS19 (lumbar puncture), and Association of British Neurologists guidance on LP safety in anticoagulation and raised intracranial pressure.
