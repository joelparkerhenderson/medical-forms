The Confusion Assessment Method is not a numeric score: delirium is classified as present when feature 1 AND feature 2 AND (feature 3 OR feature 4) are satisfied, and absent otherwise. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

Choose the CAM-ICU variant for ventilated or non-verbal patients: it uses non-verbal tasks and a RASS arousal screen. A patient who is unrousable (RASS −4 or −5) is recorded as unable to assess. CAM is a screening aid and does not replace a full clinical diagnosis of delirium and the search for its cause.

The Confusion Assessment Method (Inouye et al., Ann Intern Med 1990) and its CAM-ICU variant (Ely et al., JAMA 2001) are validated delirium screening instruments. This tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; assess, escalate, and treat in line with NICE CG103, SIGN 157, and local delirium policy alongside clinical judgement.
