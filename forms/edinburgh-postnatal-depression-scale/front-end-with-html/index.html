Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale (EPDS)

A 10-item self-report screen for perinatal depression. Each item scores 0-3 for a total of 0-30.

Please choose the answer that comes closest to how you have felt in the past 7 days, not just how you feel today. There are no right or wrong answers. Each item shows the points it contributes so the scoring is fully transparent; some items are reverse-scored, so the option order differs between items. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

The EPDS is a screen, not a diagnosis. A total of 10 or more suggests possible depression and 13 or more is a more specific threshold; either prompts further assessment. Item 10 asks about thoughts of self-harm: any answer other than "Never" prompts an immediate safety review regardless of the total. If you are in crisis, contact your midwife, health visitor, GP, or local emergency services now.

The Edinburgh Postnatal Depression Scale (Cox, Holden & Sagovsky, British Journal of Psychiatry 1987) is a validated perinatal mental-health screen. This tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; assess, escalate, and refer in line with NICE CG192, local perinatal mental-health and safeguarding policy, and clinical judgement. Item 10 must be reviewed in every case, regardless of the total.
