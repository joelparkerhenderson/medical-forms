This assessment is governed by the Mental Health Act 1983 (as amended by the Mental Health Act 2007) and its Code of Practice (2015); the Human Rights Act 1998 (Article 5, right to liberty) and the Mental Capacity Act 2005 are directly relevant. This tool documents and validates the assessment and makes no automated detention decision. The statutory forms prescribed under the Mental Health (Hospital, Guardianship and Treatment) (England) Regulations remain the definitive legal record.
