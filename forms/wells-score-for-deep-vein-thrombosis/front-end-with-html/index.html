Bedside pre-test probability rule: nine criteria (+1 each) minus 2 for an alternative diagnosis, scored −2 to 9.

Complete the Wells DVT criteria below for an adult (18 years or over) with a clinically suspected lower-limb DVT. Each of the nine criteria scores 1 point when present; subtract 2 points when an alternative diagnosis is at least as likely as DVT, for a total of −2 to 9. A score of 2 or more means DVT likely (proximal leg vein ultrasound); 1 or less means DVT unlikely (D-dimer). Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

The Wells score directs the diagnostic pathway; it does not diagnose or exclude DVT on its own. It is not validated for suspected pulmonary embolism, upper-limb DVT, or pregnancy — use the appropriate pathway and clinical judgement.

The Wells DVT score was derived and validated by Wells et al. (Lancet 1997; NEJM 2003) and is the pre-test probability tool recommended by NICE NG158. This tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; investigate, anticoagulate, and escalate in line with local policy and clinical judgement.
