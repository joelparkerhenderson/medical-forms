Complete this annual health check for a person aged 14 or over on the practice learning-disability register. Record every required component with a finding — and, where a problem is found, an action — then produce and share a Health Action Plan the person can keep. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

This is a documentation and completeness aid, not a severity grade or a diagnosis. The check is Complete only when every required component was carried out and a Health Action Plan was produced and shared; otherwise it is Incomplete. Clinical flags (including STOMP) are raised independently to prompt follow-up.

This annual health check follows the NHS England annual health check programme, the RCGP / Cardiff University health check template, and the STOMP / STAMP national programmes. It is a documentation and completeness aid for information only, and is not a diagnosis or a severity grade — use clinical judgement and act on the flags and Health Action Plan in line with local policy and the NHS Accessible Information Standard.
