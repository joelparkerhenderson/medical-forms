Enter the laboratory inputs for an adult with chronic liver disease. The calculator converts the values to mg/dL, applies the dialysis creatinine rule and the value bounds, computes the weighted logarithmic MELD score, and maps it to an estimated 3-month mortality band. Choose the variant (MELD, MELD-Na, or MELD 3.0) in step 1; the sodium and albumin steps appear only when the chosen variant needs them. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

The score summarises mortality risk and informs transplant prioritisation; it does not diagnose the underlying liver disease and does not replace clinical judgement. Values below 1.0 are raised to 1.0 before the logarithm, creatinine is capped at 4.0 mg/dL, and the final score is clamped to 6–40.

MELD was derived by Malinchoc et al. (Hepatology 2000) and adopted by UNOS in 2002; MELD-Na (Kim et al., NEJM 2008) adds serum sodium and MELD 3.0 (Kim et al., Gastroenterology 2021) adds sex and albumin. This calculator is a decision-support aid for information only and is not a diagnosis; interpret in the clinical context and use local allocation policy where it differs.
