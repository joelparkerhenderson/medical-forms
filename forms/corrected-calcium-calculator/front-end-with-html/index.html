Enter the measured total calcium (mmol/L) and serum albumin (g/L) for an adult. The calculator applies the standard albumin-correction formula and reports a corrected calcium value, classified against the adult reference range 2.20–2.60 mmol/L. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

The corrected value is an estimate that improves interpretation when albumin is abnormal; it is not a substitute for a measured ionised calcium and does not replace clinical judgement. Severe results (≥ 3.0 or < 1.9 mmol/L) are flagged for urgent action.

Submit the form to see the report.

The albumin-correction formula is drawn from Payne et al. (BMJ 1973) and the Association for Clinical Biochemistry and Laboratory Medicine (ACB) guidance on adjusted calcium. This calculator is an interpretation aid for information only and is not a diagnosis; interpret in the clinical context and use local laboratory reference ranges where they differ.
