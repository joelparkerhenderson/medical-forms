The Anaesthetic Record is a contemporaneous intra-operative clinical record. It follows RCoA GPAS anaesthesia-records guidance, the Association of Anaesthetists monitoring standards (2021), the WHO Surgical Safety Checklist (2009), the ASA physical-status classification, and Cormack–Lehane grading. This tool is for documentation and completeness-checking only and is not a diagnosis; clinical decisions rest with the anaesthetist.
