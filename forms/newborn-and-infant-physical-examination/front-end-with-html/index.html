Each of the four key screening components — eyes, heart, hips, and testes (in boys) — is classified as Satisfactory, Refer, or Not examined, and the overall screening outcome and referral pathways are computed from them. NIPE is a screening instrument, not a diagnosis. A satisfactory screen is not a guarantee of health, and a Refer result is a prompt to escalate along a defined pathway within a defined timeframe. Use clinical judgement alongside the NIPE programme standards.

The Newborn and Infant Physical Examination (NIPE) is a UK National Screening Committee programme (Public Health England / NHS England NIPE Screening Programme Handbook; NICE NG194). This tool documents findings and prompts referral; it is for information only and is not a diagnosis. Escalate and refer in line with the NIPE programme standards and clinical judgement.
