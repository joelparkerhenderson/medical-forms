Complete the four 4AT items below for an adult in whom delirium or cognitive impairment is suspected or should be routinely screened. Items 1 and 4 score 0 or 4 points; items 2 and 3 score 0, 1, or 2. The four item scores sum to a total of 0 to 12. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

The 4AT is a screening aid, not a diagnosis. A score of 4 or more indicates possible delirium and should prompt a full clinical assessment against DSM-5 or ICD-10 criteria. A score of 0 does not exclude delirium if the acute-change information (item 4) could not be reliably obtained.

The 4AT (4 'A's Test) is a validated rapid screening instrument for delirium and cognitive impairment (MacLullich et al.; Bellelli et al., Age and Ageing 2014; Shenkin et al., BMC Medicine 2019) recommended by NICE CG103 and SIGN 157. This screening tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; a positive result should trigger a full clinical assessment in line with local policy and clinical judgement.
