The engine does not compute a clinical score — it checks whether the referral is complete enough to be acted on, reports a completeness percentage, classifies the urgency (emergency / urgent under Children Act 1989 s47, or standard under s17), and raises safeguarding flags. The urgency is shown even while the referral is incomplete so that danger is never hidden. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

This tool records and routes a child safeguarding referral under the Children Act 1989 (s17 child in need, s47 significant harm) and Working Together to Safeguard Children (2023). It is an administrative documentation and workflow-routing aid; it does not diagnose or treat, and it does not replace real-time professional judgement. The consent and information-sharing basis follows the Information Sharing advice for practitioners (2018).
