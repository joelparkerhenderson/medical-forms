Complete the eight ReSPECT sections below through a conversation with the person (and, where they lack capacity, those close to them). ReSPECT records recommendations, not legally binding instructions. The engine does not compute a clinical score — it checks whether the plan is complete and valid, reports a completeness percentage, and raises safety and governance flags. Answers are saved automatically in your browser as you go.

A ReSPECT plan does not replace real-time clinical judgement in an emergency, and is not consent to a specific procedure. Legally binding refusals belong in an Advance Decision to Refuse Treatment.

ReSPECT (Recommended Summary Plan for Emergency Care and Treatment) is developed and maintained by the Resuscitation Council UK (version 3.0). This tool records and validates clinician-agreed recommendations; it is for information only and is not a diagnosis. The capacity and involvement rules reflect the Mental Capacity Act 2005 (in Scotland, the Adults with Incapacity (Scotland) Act 2000).
