Use this checklist while observing a trainee perform First Aid at Work (FAW) skills aligned with UK HSE guidance and the St John Ambulance syllabus. For each item, mark whether the trainee demonstrated the skill correctly, has not yet demonstrated it, or whether the item was not assessed in this session. Critical life-saving skills are highlighted; any critical-skill failure results in an overall Fail.

Outcome criteria: Pass — all skills demonstrated to a competent standard. Needs Development — minor (1-2) non-critical deficiencies; targeted retraining recommended. Fail — critical deficiency in a life-saving skill, or three or more non-critical deficiencies. Answers are saved automatically to your browser as you go.

The First Aid at Work (FAW) Competency Assessment is a competency-based instrument used to certify rescuers under UK Health & Safety Executive (HSE) guidance, aligned with the St John Ambulance syllabus. This examiner tool records observed performance and applies HSE/St John pass / needs-development / fail criteria; it is not a substitute for the instructor's professional judgement.
