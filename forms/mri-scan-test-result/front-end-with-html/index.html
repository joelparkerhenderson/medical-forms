This structured MRI report is aligned with Royal College of Radiologists (RCR) reporting standards, including the five mandatory report sections (clinical history, sequences/technique, comparison, findings, impression), critical-result communication per actionable-reporting guidance, and structured MRI reporting systems such as ACR PI-RADS (prostate) and BI-RADS (breast). It is a reporting aid, not a diagnosis; interpret and act in line with local radiology pathways and clinical judgement.
