Complete the four Centor criteria below for a patient aged 3 years or over with an acute sore throat. Each criterion scores 1 point when present, for a Centor total of 0 to 4. The McIsaac age modifier (+1 for ages 3–14, 0 for 15–44, −1 for 45 and over) adjusts this to a modified score of −1 to 5 that guides testing and antibiotic decisions. The score is a decision aid, not a diagnosis. It never overrides clinical judgement or a red flag for airway compromise or peritonsillar abscess (quinsy). NICE NG84 recommends Centor or FeverPAIN for acute sore throat.

The Centor score (Centor et al., Medical Decision Making 1981) with the McIsaac age modification (McIsaac et al., CMAJ 1998; JAMA 2004) estimates the probability of group A streptococcal pharyngitis and guides testing and antibiotic decisions under antimicrobial-stewardship principles (NICE NG84). This tool is for information only and is not a diagnosis; use clinical judgement and act on any airway or quinsy red flag irrespective of the score.
