Clinician wizard for a Statement of Fitness for Work after illness, injury, surgery, a mental-health condition, or extended absence. Record the assessing clinician, the employee, the job context (including any safety-critical or DVLA-notifiable status), the absence history, the clinical reason and mechanism, current treatment, a functional assessment, the overall fitness outcome and validity period, any phased return, the enumerated workplace adjustments and restrictions, the follow-up plan, and the clinician sign-off. The engine computes a fitness statement, a restriction-priority grade (the most severe fired adjustment sets the grade), and a set of occupational-health safety flags.

This is clinical decision support. The clinician may override the computed fitness statement with a documented reason on the sign-off step; both the computed and final statements are recorded.

Submit the form to see the statement of fitness.

Aligned with the UK DWP Statement of Fitness for Work (Med 3) guidance for healthcare professionals, the fit-note guidance for patients and employees, Acas return-to-work guidance, HSE RIDDOR, DVLA assessing fitness to drive, and the Equality Act 2010 reasonable-adjustment duty. This tool is for clinical decision support; the responsible clinician makes the final determination.
