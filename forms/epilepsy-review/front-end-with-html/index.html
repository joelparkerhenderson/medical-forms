This is a control-classification and documentation-completeness instrument, not a numeric score. It classifies seizure control as seizure-free / controlled / uncontrolled, grades the review Complete / Partial / Incomplete, and raises safety flags — most importantly valproate in a woman of childbearing potential without a pregnancy-prevention programme, status epilepticus, and driving while not DVLA-eligible. It does not diagnose epilepsy or select medication.

The annual review follows NICE NG217 (Epilepsies in children, young people and adults), MHRA valproate guidance, and DVLA fitness-to-drive rules. This tool documents findings, classifies seizure control, and prompts escalation; it is not a diagnosis and does not replace clinical judgement. A "seizure-free" classification does not by itself authorise driving or medication withdrawal — those remain clinical and DVLA decisions. Where a high-priority flag is raised (valproate without a pregnancy-prevention programme, status epilepticus, or driving while not DVLA-eligible), act on it promptly.
